Foundation

Brand Language

L'OISEAU DÉ is a modern atelier — a space where elegance meets simplicity. The design system reflects a botanical luxury sensibility: nature-sourced, unhurried, and deeply considered.

A

Botanical Palette

Colors drawn from nature — forest greens, sage, cream, warm espresso — with lime chartreuse as the living accent that bridges earth and energy.

B

Editorial Scale

Display typography used at dramatic scale. "Clash Display" carries the brand personality — geometric, confident, and unhurried. Never crowded.

C

Restraint

White space is the primary luxury. The grid breathes. No element competes with the product. Decoration is used only when it serves the product story.

D

Sensory Language

Copy uses texture words: velvet, warm, fresh, radiant. UI language matches this register — never dry or transactional. Product names carry emotion.

Motion

Interaction

Transitions are subtle and deliberate. The brand moves at the pace of a curated atelier — not a flash sale. Nothing bounces; everything eases.

Default Transition
all 0.18s ease
Hover states on buttons, links, and interactive elements.
Fade In
opacity 0.3s ease-out
Page-level content reveals and modal overlays. Opacity only.
Slide Up
transform 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
Hero text entrance. Only on first load, never on repeated patterns.
Reduced Motion
prefers-reduced-motion: reduce
All transitions disabled. Motion is enhancement only.
